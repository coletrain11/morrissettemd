Swing Cam

Level
Not connected

Place the cameras

Both phones go at the height of your hands at address, upright and level. Down the line sits behind you on your hand line. Face-on sits in front of you, square to the target line. Set them up the same way every session so swings compare cleanly.

DTL

Down the line

  1. Lay a club on the floor along your toes, pointing at the target. Your hand line runs parallel to it, a little inside, under your hands.
  2. Put the phone about 10 ft behind the ball (8–12 ft works), directly on that hand line, pointed straight at the screen.
  3. Set the lens at the height of your hands at address, roughly belt height. Keep the phone upright and level; the level dot on the phone turns green.
  4. Leave room in the frame: the club at the top of your backswing stays below the top yellow line, and your feet sit near the bottom line.

Looks right when

  • Your hands sit on the center line at address.
  • The shaft at address and the target line look like they point at the same spot on the screen.
FO

Face-on

  1. Lay a club on the floor from the middle of your stance straight toward the camera spot, square to the target line.
  2. Put the phone at the end of that club line, about 8 ft from the ball (6–12 ft works), facing your chest.
  3. Set the lens at hand height, upright and level, the same height as the DTL phone.
  4. Frame yourself a little below center: room above for the club at the top, room below to see the ball.

Looks right when

  • The center line runs through the middle of your stance.
  • Your feet look the same size and the floor looks flat, not slanted.

Check it on the iPad

  1. Turn on LIVE on the iPad. The same aiming lines appear over both views, so you can adjust each phone while you watch.
  2. Take a practice swing and pause at the top of the backswing. The whole club should be in both frames.
  3. Mark each tripod spot with tape so next time takes a minute.
Shanks fly toward the face-on phone. Put it behind the bay's side net if there is one, or keep it at least 8 ft away.

Set up the bay

Get all three devices online first (see Network below), then scan each code with the iPhone for that angle.

Room
····

Each phone listens for the sound of impact. Set its trigger line just above the room noise, then make a practice hit. If it misses, lower the line.

Network: bay Wi-Fi

  1. Join the iPad and both iPhones to the same Wi-Fi.
  2. Scan the codes above. Each camera card shows how it connected.
  3. “Local network” is best. If a camera says “Relay”, or never connects, the Wi-Fi blocks devices from talking to each other. Use a hotspot instead.

Network: no usable Wi-Fi

  1. Turn on Personal Hotspot on one iPhone (it can be one of the cameras).
  2. Join the iPad and the other iPhone to that hotspot. Video stays on the hotspot; only the brief handshake uses cellular data.
  3. If devices can't share a hotspot, each can use its own cellular data with a relay server below.

Relay server (optional)

For cellular-only setups. Paste the “ICE servers array” from a free Metered Open Relay account. It's saved on this iPad and carried to the phones in the QR codes.

Google Drive (optional)

Uploads exported videos, and optionally every swing's original clips, to a “Swing Cam” folder in your Drive, one folder per day. It needs a free Google OAuth client ID for this site, a one-time setup. Without it, use Export → Save to Photos / Share → Save to Files → Google Drive.

  1. At console.cloud.google.com, create a project and enable the Google Drive API.
  2. OAuth consent screen: External, and add your Google account as a test user.
  3. Credentials → Create OAuth client ID → Web application. Authorized JavaScript origin: https://morrissettemd.com
  4. Paste the client ID here and tap Connect.
